Read the map responsibly

What this data can—and cannot—tell us

01

County layer

The local map uses 2020 Feeding America Map the Meal Gap modeled estimates hosted by the federal HRSA. It combines survey, Census, unemployment, and food-cost inputs.

02

Current context

Statewide cards use newer UCLA CHIS and California Food Banks findings. Their populations and methods differ, so they should not be compared directly with the map.

03

Use with local knowledge

This is a prioritization starting point. Confirm conditions with food banks and communities before allocating services or funding.
